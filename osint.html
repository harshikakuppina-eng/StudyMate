OSINT 🕵️

Learn how publicly available information can be collected, verified and analyzed safely.

What is OSINT?

OSINT stands for Open Source Intelligence.

It is the process of collecting and analyzing information that is publicly available.

Cybersecurity professionals use OSINT during authorized security assessments and investigations.

Common OSINT Sources 🌐

  • Search engines
  • Public websites
  • Public social-media information
  • Public documents
  • Domain and DNS information
  • Publicly available metadata

Basic OSINT Process 🔎

  1. Define what information you need.
  2. Collect information from public sources.
  3. Verify the information.
  4. Organize the collected information.
  5. Analyze the information.
  6. Document your findings.

Simple Example 💡

Suppose a security professional is assessing their own website.

They may check publicly available information such as the website's domain, DNS records, technologies used and public documents.

The goal is to understand what information is publicly exposed and improve security.

OSINT Safety Rules 🛡️

  • Use only public information.
  • Respect privacy and applicable laws.
  • Do not bypass authentication.
  • Do not access private accounts.
  • Practice only on systems you own or have permission to test.

Key Points ⭐

  • OSINT means Open Source Intelligence.
  • It uses publicly available information.
  • Information should be verified before using it.
  • OSINT is useful in cybersecurity investigations.
  • Always follow legal and ethical boundaries.
