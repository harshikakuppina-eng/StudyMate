Cybersecurity Basics 🔐

Learn the basic concepts of cybersecurity and how digital systems are protected.

🛡️ What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications and data from unauthorized access, attacks and damage.

Example: Using a strong password and two-factor authentication helps protect your online account.

🔺 CIA Triad

The CIA Triad is one of the most important concepts in cybersecurity.

1. Confidentiality 🔒

Only authorized people should be able to access information.

2. Integrity 📝

Information should not be changed or modified without authorization.

3. Availability ⚡

Systems and information should be available when authorized users need them.

⚠️ Common Cyber Threats

  • Phishing: Fake messages or websites used to trick users.
  • Malware: Malicious software designed to harm or misuse systems.
  • Password Attacks: Attempts to gain unauthorized access to accounts.
  • Social Engineering: Manipulating people to reveal information.

🛡️ Basic Protection Methods

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
  • Keep software and operating systems updated.
  • Do not open suspicious links or attachments.
  • Use trusted security software.
  • Take regular backups of important data.

💡 Simple Example

Imagine your Instagram account is protected by a strong password and MFA.

Even if someone somehow obtains your password, MFA can provide an additional security layer.

📌 Key Points to Remember

  • Cybersecurity protects digital systems and data.
  • CIA means Confidentiality, Integrity and Availability.
  • Phishing and malware are common cyber threats.
  • Strong passwords and MFA improve security.
  • Security is everyone's responsibility.
