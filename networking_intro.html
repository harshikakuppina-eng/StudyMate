🌐 Introduction to Computer Networks

Learn what a computer network is, why networks are used and how devices communicate with each other.

💻 What is a Computer Network?

A computer network is a group of computers and other devices connected together so that they can communicate and share resources.

Example: Your laptop connected to Wi-Fi can communicate with your router and access the Internet.

🎯 Why Do We Need Networks?

✔ Share files

✔ Share printers

✔ Access the Internet

✔ Communicate with other devices

✔ Share applications and resources

🔗 Basic Network Example

A simple home network can contain:

💻 Laptop → 📡 Wi-Fi Router → 🌐 Internet

The router acts as a connection point between your devices and the Internet.

📚 Types of Networks

LAN — Local Area Network

A network covering a small area such as a home, classroom or office.

MAN — Metropolitan Area Network

A network that covers a larger area such as a city.

WAN — Wide Area Network

A network covering a very large geographical area. The Internet is the best-known example.

📦 Network Components

Common components include:

💻 Computer

📱 Mobile

📡 Router

🔀 Switch

🔌 Network cables

🌐 Network protocols

🔄 How Communication Happens

When one device wants to communicate with another device, data is divided into smaller units called packets.

These packets travel through the network and are reassembled at the destination.

🛡️ Importance in Cybersecurity

Understanding networks is very important in cybersecurity because security professionals need to understand how devices communicate.

Concepts such as IP addresses, ports, protocols and packets are used throughout cybersecurity.

📝 Key Points

• Network = connected devices

• Networks allow communication and resource sharing

• LAN covers a small area

• MAN covers a city-sized area

• WAN covers a large geographical area

• Data travels through the network as packets
